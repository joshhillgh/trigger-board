Welcome to the Trigger Board

A weekly, source-cited buying-trigger sweep for your patch, run by Claude Code, rendered as a live board. The Insight AI offer and plays are already baked in — you just set who you are and the industry you sell into. Two minutes, and everything you enter stays on this machine.

1 Who you are

Used to sign your outreach drafts and frame the sweep. Only your name and patch are required.

2 Your ICP band

The sweep disqualifies hard — that's the point. Agree these with whoever owns your playbook.

3 Your industry's sources

The one part that really changes per industry. The primary source should be a dated daily or weekly news digest the sweep can walk edition by edition — every industry has one. Not sure? Leave it blank and ask Claude to suggest one on your first sweep.

4 Your playbook

The sweep follows your signed-off playbook rather than improvising. It is saved to this folder only and is git-ignored, so it can never end up in the shared repo.

Drop your playbook here or click to choose a file Word document (.docx) — saved locally as playbook.docx

No playbook yet? You can save without one and drop it in later — the sweep will ask for it.

You're set up

  1. Start your first sweep: open this folder in Claude Code and paste:
    Run this week's buying-trigger sweep per CLAUDE.md.
  2. Make it automatic: in Claude Code, say "Run the buying-trigger sweep every Monday morning." — it can schedule itself.
  3. Open your board: dashboard.html — it fills with real accounts after the first sweep. Re-run setup any time at setup.html.
